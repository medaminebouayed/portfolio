Platana — Industrial ML Decision Engine

AI & Full-Stack — Summer Internship

Platana is a SaaS platform for after-sales service digitalization. As part of my Summer Internship, I designed and developed an ML-based decision-support engine that grades the condition of a returned product from multi-angle photos, estimates its resale price and refurbishment cost, and recommends a disposition (direct resale, refurbishment, or recycling). The system also generates a ready-to-use product listing and enforces human validation with full decision traceability. The goal is to reduce value loss and increase the share of reused products within a circular-economy approach. I worked on a real, noisy industrial dataset: feature engineering, PyTorch and XGBoost model training, validation against expert ground truth, and structured documentation of model limits and error patterns. The architecture includes a FastAPI backend, Redis cache, Angular frontend, MLflow experiment tracking, and Docker + Traefik + Nginx deployment.

PythonFastAPIPyTorch/ONNXXGBoostMLflowPandasNumPyDockerAngularRedisTraefikNginx

SmartCampus AI — Agentic Orientation Platform

Agentic AI & LLMs

SmartCampus AI is a multi-step conversational Agentic AI pipeline built with LangGraph. The agent interacts with users via text and audio, progressively builds the candidate profile through structured dialogue, queries university documents via Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), generates adaptive QCMs based on the profile, and orchestrates pre-registration workflows with human-in-the-loop validation. I designed tool-use patterns connecting the agent to document stores, profile generators, and a workflow engine. The architecture includes FastAPI for the backend, Next.js for the frontend, Whisper for audio transcription, open-source LLMs, and FAISS for vector retrieval.
